Pizza Margherita Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 pizza dough (store-bought or homemade)
  • 1/2 cup San Marzano tomato sauce
  • 200g fresh mozzarella cheese, sliced
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Steps:

  1. Preheat your oven to the highest temperature, typically around 250°C (480°F). If using a pizza stone, place it in the oven while preheating.
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface to your desired thickness.
  3. Spread the San Marzano tomato sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border around the edges.
  4. Top the sauce with slices of fresh mozzarella cheese.
  5. Add fresh basil leaves and drizzle olive oil over the pizza.
  6. Sprinkle a pinch of salt over the toppings.
  7. Bake the pizza in the preheated oven for 7–10 minutes, or until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly.
  8. Let the pizza cool slightly before slicing and serving.
Pasta Carbonara Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 200g spaghetti or pasta of your choice
  • 100g guanciale or pancetta, diced
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup grated Pecorino Romano cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • Salt, for pasta water
  • 1 tbsp olive oil (optional)

Steps:

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta according to the package instructions until al dente.
  2. While the pasta is cooking, heat a pan over medium heat and cook the guanciale (or pancetta) until crispy, about 5–7 minutes. Remove from heat and set aside.
  3. In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, Pecorino Romano, Parmigiano-Reggiano, and a generous amount of freshly ground black pepper.
  4. Once the pasta is cooked, reserve 1/2 cup of pasta water before draining the rest.
  5. Quickly toss the hot pasta in the pan with the guanciale, allowing the heat to slightly wilt the meat and coat the pasta with the rendered fat. If the pan is too dry, add a little bit of the reserved pasta water.
  6. Remove the pan from the heat and immediately pour the egg mixture over the pasta, tossing quickly to create a creamy sauce. The residual heat will cook the eggs without scrambling them.
  7. If the sauce is too thick, add more reserved pasta water to achieve a creamy consistency.
  8. Serve immediately, topping with more grated cheese and a generous amount of black pepper.
Lasagna Recipe

Ingredients:

  • Lasagna pasta sheets
  • 500g ground beef
  • 500g ground pork
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 cups tomato sauce
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Olive oil

Steps:

  1. Preheat your oven to 180°C (356°F).
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the onion and garlic until fragrant. Add the ground beef and pork, cooking until browned.
  3. Stir in the tomato sauce and season with salt and pepper. Let it simmer for 15–20 minutes.
  4. In a bowl, mix the ricotta cheese, egg, and half of the Parmesan cheese.
  5. In a baking dish, layer lasagna sheets, the meat sauce, the ricotta mixture, and mozzarella cheese. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, finishing with mozzarella and Parmesan cheese on top.
  6. Cover with foil and bake for 30 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 10–15 minutes until the cheese is golden and bubbly.
  7. Let it cool slightly before serving.
Tiramisu Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 6 egg yolks
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 cup mascarpone cheese
  • 1 1/2 cups heavy cream
  • 2 cups brewed espresso or instant espresso
  • 1/4 cup Marsala wine or rum
  • 1 pack ladyfinger cookies
  • Cocoa powder for dusting

Steps:

  1. Whisk the egg yolks and sugar over a pot of simmering water until thickened. Let it cool, then mix in the mascarpone cheese.
  2. In a separate bowl, whip the heavy cream until stiff peaks form and fold it into the mascarpone mixture.
  3. Combine the espresso with Marsala wine or rum in a shallow dish.
  4. Dip each ladyfinger briefly into the espresso mixture and layer them in a dish.
  5. Spread a layer of the mascarpone mixture over the cookies. Repeat the process, ending with the mascarpone layer on top.
  6. Dust the top with cocoa powder and refrigerate for at least 4–6 hours, or overnight, to set.
  7. Serve chilled.
Spagetthi Bolognese Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 500g ground beef
  • 1 carrot, finely chopped
  • 1 celery stalk, finely chopped
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 cup red wine
  • 2 cups tomato paste
  • 2 cups beef stock
  • Olive oil
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Steps:

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pot and sauté the onion, garlic, carrot, and celery until softened.
  3. Add the ground beef and cook until browned. Stir in the milk and let it simmer until absorbed.
  4. Pour in the red wine and let it reduce for 5–10 minutes.
  5. Stir in the tomato paste and beef stock, then season with salt and pepper. Simmer on low heat for 1–2 hours, stirring occasionally.
  6. Serve the sauce over the cooked spaghetti, with grated Parmesan if desired.
Pesto Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups fresh basil leaves
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/3 cup pine nuts
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Steps:

  1. In a food processor, combine basil leaves, Parmesan cheese, pine nuts, and garlic. Blend until finely chopped.
  2. Slowly drizzle in olive oil while the food processor is running, blending until smooth.
  3. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  4. Use immediately with pasta, or store in the refrigerator for up to a week.
